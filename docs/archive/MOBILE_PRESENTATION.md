# Mobile prototype presentation

Team downloads: **https://wasl-team-prototype-oct26.helloworldd.chatgpt.site/mobile.html**

The existing Wasl app adapts to a phone screen with bottom navigation for Network, Cards, Recall, Profile, and Settings. Open the shared HTTPS demo on your phone and tap **Open presentation demo**. This is a mobile web prototype; it does not require an App Store installation.

The download page contains:

- An approximately 80-second silent MP4 of actual interactions in a 390 × 844 phone viewport, exported at 780 × 1688 in H.264 for presentation use.
- Twelve portrait PNG screenshots covering the main pages and the sharing, receiving, notes, recall, capture and review flow.
- A complete ZIP and optional narration text.

For the presentation, insert the MP4 in a slide and talk over it, or use the phone screenshots alongside your explanation. The video and files remain available independently of the presenter's laptop. The interactive demo still requires the Mac to remain online; see [team demo setup](TEAM_DEMO.md).

All recorded data is fictional, captured in a separate recording workspace. The recording demonstrates real saved contacts, private notes, keyword recall and editable pasted-text capture. Live AI recall and photo scanning are not connected. Local QR links visible in the recording are illustrative; generate QR links on the HTTPS team demo for use across devices.
